The Builds

Things she made because the work needed them.

She has no formal training in code. She taught herself to build because the manual version of the work kept letting people down, and nobody asked her to. Each of these began as a problem someone on the team kept running into.

01

AccessTrack

The ESP learner portal

One place for a seven-week training programme. Learners find their sessions, assignments and progress there, and the people running it grade work, issue certificates and report on each cohort. It took over from a tangle of spreadsheets, WhatsApp threads and email, and now belongs to the programme’s Training Lead.

02

Scout

For MHCI’s ventures

Finds funding opportunities and scores how well each one fits the studio’s ventures, so the team spends its time on the applications worth writing.

03

MHCI Brain

For the whole studio

A shared home for how the studio works, with maps of the organisation, its procedures and its contacts. One corner, Grapevine, keeps the informal stories that never survive in a procedure. She thinks of it as closer to a zine than a wiki.

04

TalentWorks Portal

For TalentWorks

Follows every executive assistant from the roster through to a placement, and helps match each client’s brief to the right person.

05

Brief

For the founder

Sorts the founder’s inbox by urgency and sends one summary each morning.

Also on the bench: a candidate dossier generator for TalentWorks, the application and screening system for TechRise Women, and the automation behind her weekly reports.
