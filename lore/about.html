About

Polymath from the Niger Delta

Ikanke-Abasi Umana is a programs and operations professional, researcher, writer, and event host based in Akwa Ibom State, Nigeria. She studied Communication Arts at the University of Uyo.

She currently serves as Product Integration & Growth Manager and TalentWorks Venture Lead at Mosaic Human Capital Innovations (MHCI), building operational systems, leading product-market strategy, running the TalentWorks talent placement venture, designing training curricula, and managing multi-stakeholder partnerships. This year that includes leading the TechRise Women Initiative and Akwa Ibom Tech Week 2026. She is also Co-Founder of Founder Advocacy Network (FAN).

She has been hosting events since she was a child: children’s day programmes and teens conventions at her parents’ church, Model UN director in secondary school, to tech festival stages and founder meetups. Her range is wide by design. When something needs doing, she steps forward.

Beyond the Work

She is also this.

Fashion & Design

Clothes are another language

She is deeply interested in fashion as expression and identity. She makes clothes, including what she wore to both editions of Church Clothes, and pieces made for independent creative projects. She has a line of graphic tees in development. Her interest is in how fashion communicates identity, particularly within faith and cultural contexts.

Music & Voice

Singer. Choir director.

She has directed teen choirs and loves gospel music and anything with great storytelling. Music and language feel like the same act, both ways of organising feeling into form. Favourite podcast: Hasan Minhaj Doesn’t Know.

Politics & Discourse

Interested in how power works

Has a genuine interest in politics, governance, and the infrastructure of public discourse, and writes about these things in her essays. Enjoys solitaire, tea, and cold pineapple. Has a tendency toward flamboyant word use and considers it a feature.
