Work with me

I help organisations design programmes and run them well.

Most of my work happens where an idea has to become something people show up for: a programme, an event, or a team that needs a better way of working. I plan it, set up the systems behind it, and stay with it until it runs.

Send me an email →See what I offer

Based in Akwa Ibom State, Nigeria. Available in person in Akwa Ibom and remotely elsewhere.

How I work

Three things I always do.

01

Start with the people it is for.

Every programme or event I design starts with who it serves and what they should walk away with.

02

Build the system before the rush.

I set up the plan, the tracker and the roles early, so everyone can see what is done, what is behind and who owns it.

03

Write it down for whoever comes next.

I leave behind documents and tools the next person can use, so the work keeps going without me in the room.
