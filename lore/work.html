Work

Programmes and operations in Akwa Ibom.

Ikanke-Abasi Umana is a programmes and operations professional based in Uyo and Eket, Akwa Ibom State, Nigeria. She is Project Manager for Akwa Ibom Tech Week 2026, and Venture Lead for TalentWorks and Product Integration & Growth Manager at Mosaic Human Capital Innovations (MHCI).

The Work

Four rooms, one address.

By day she runs talent programmes, builds learner portals, leads product-market strategy, and coordinates government and NGO partnerships across a multi-venture consultancy. By evening she chairs founder meetups, writes essays, and designs event collateral. The scope is who she is.

01

Programs & Operations

At MHCI, she built TalentWorks from an idea into a working placement venture, and now looks after a growing roster of 25 executive assistants placed with clients in Abuja, Uyo, Enugu and beyond. She designed the TechRise Women Initiative for 250 women with the Office of the First Lady (Akwa Ibom State) and AfriLabs, negotiated the agreement between the three, and now leads its delivery through three partner organisations. When a process keeps tripping people up, she tends to build the tool that fixes it. Her builds have a room of their own. At Zitere Social Enterprises (Triift Africa), she managed the HOME Project in Ogun State. She is Co-Founder of Founder Advocacy Network (FAN).

02

Research & Analysis

Her undergraduate seminar research on gender representation in UNICEF’s X posts is in the process of being published in an academic journal, co-authored with her university lecturer. The study found stories centring boys were systematically underrepresented. Her final-year thesis studied how ThisDay and The Punch covered Nigeria’s 2023 fuel subsidy removal. She also writes analytical memos connecting policy and economic data to organisational decisions.

03

Writing & Voice

Founder of On This Note, her commentary on politics, culture and the economy, and the particular texture of being Nigerian and alive. First invited by Jollof & Fiction to speak on Pluralism on their podcast; they later reviewed her essay. Audio companion series VoiceNotes on Spotify. Three years hosting Teens’ Time with Chichi on Inspiration FM 105.9.

04

Events & Ecosystem Building

Patron and lead designer for Founders Friday Uyo, a founder community that has met every month since April 2025. It now counts more than 120 members across three towns, with LGA editions alongside Mark Essien (Eket) and Future Labs (IK), and its founders have gone on to win over ₦3.5 million in investment and pitch prizes. She also writes and designs its opportunities newsletter, the Founders Friday Uyo Digest. She consulted on programme design for Akwa Ibom Tech Week 2025, where she conceived the GIFA First Lady’s Breakfast for Women in Tech as an event of its own, the first of its kind in the state, and she is Project Manager for the 2026 edition. Judge at WTM Uyo IWD 2026. Has hosted over 30 events across church stages, university festivals, tech conferences, and private occasions.
